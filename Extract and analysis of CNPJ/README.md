# Analysis CNPJ informations of customers

## Objectives
- Extract CNPJ information fom customers to obtain datas about localization, company size, share capital, shareholder structure, registration status, main economic activity code and legal nature;
- Analyze this data to structure the customers profile;
- Identify correlations between different customer attributes to gain deeper insights into their behaviors and characteristics;
- Apply clustering techniques to group customers based on similar profiles, enabling targeted analysis and personalized strategies;
- Develop actionable insights to support decision-making and improve customer segmentation strategies and marketshare.

### Advanced step
- Analyze the correlation between customer profiles and billing size to identify patterns and trends that can support strategic decision-making.
